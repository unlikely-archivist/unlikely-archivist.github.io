Creative Technologist

I build automations, fictional worlds, browser applications, and more. My recent work includes a newsletter that writes, fact-checks, and publishes itself; a social media scheduler whose captions sound like you; and browser tools for design that I wished existed.

I’ve spent a decade in the film industry running prop departments, so I’m excellent with budgets, deadlines, and teamwork. However, the true talent I carry over is anticipating the next request, problem, or outcome, and always being ready for it.
