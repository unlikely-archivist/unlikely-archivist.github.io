Eryn Montgomery

Creative Technologist & Automation Engineer

I build things with code. Some useful, some for entertainment.

Automation, browser applications, human-supervised AI systems, and interactive fictional worlds. My recent work includes a publication that researches, fact-checks, and publishes itself; a social media scheduler that drafts its own captions and never posts without approval; and tools for creative design.

Before this, I spent a decade running prop departments in the film industry, managing budgets, crews, vendors, and deadlines, but most importantly anticipating what other people were going to want or do, and what could possibly go wrong. I bring that same lens to technology: identify potential problems, anticipate behavior (human and machine), and cover my @$$.
